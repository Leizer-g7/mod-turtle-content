Max's patch-z copy must be rebuilt: take this table and add his 16 modified Arathi rows and his rows 3111-3113.
- `Textures\Minimap\md5translate.trs` and the minimap images - patch-z holds 96 images and a trs. This build ships 261 tile images of its own (`Textures\Minimap\<Dir>\mapX_Y.blp`, the same naming as Max's) and a full trs with a `dir: Giln` block. Compare in game, then drop the patch-z images/trs or keep them.
- ADT tiles - patch-z holds about 140 Azeroth/Kalimdor tiles. All shipped tiles listed below changed in patch-y; the patch-z copies hide them where both exist.
- World map art (`.zmp`, continent BLPs, Arathi art) - not built by the converter; Max keeps them in patch-z.
